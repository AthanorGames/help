Terminology ​

Unit (umbrella term): any character on the battlefield

  • Soldier: regular combat unit under a hero's command (warrior, rogue, or alchemist)
  • Hero: leadership unit that can lead a party (Lord, Champion, Beastmaster)
  • Creature: tamed beasts (Chimera, Salamander, Basilisk, Quicksilver Elemental, Brass Golem)
  • Homunculus: wild creature spawns; cannot be tamed

Art / Skill / Working (all the same thing): any ability a hero learns and uses

  • Passive: always on (Rally, Shield Wall, Beast Bond)
  • Active: used on the battlefield, costs an action (Transmute Blast, Ensnare, Shadow Step)
  • Map ability: used between fights, once per day (Mend the Column, Set Trap)

Building / Holding (same thing): any structure on the map

  • City: large settlement you control; builds new structures, researches skills
  • Grand Athanor: massive city at the world's heart; capturing it wins the game
  • Farmstead: field that produces food
  • Lair: home to a creature; capture it to hire that beast

Movement

  • Move: an on-battlefield step to an adjacent tile (tactical)
  • March: travel across the world map; a party marches from tile to tile across several days

Guild Resources: what your treasury holds

  • It is gold or 🪙, never "coin"
  • Stores: the pooled food, elixir, and reagents your guild holds

Quick Learner's Path ​

First Campaign (25 min to first victory):

  1. Pick your Lord: Warrior (tanks), Rogue (speed), or Alchemist (magic)
  2. Pick your guild: choose one for the home terrain and perk you like
  3. First goal: Liberate your Grand Athanor nearby (the tutorial shows you how)
  4. Core loop each day:
    • Move your parties to attack or defend
    • End day → pay your army → repeat
  5. Long goal: Capture Entropy's Grand Athanor at the world's center to win

Expand safely:

  • Capture buildings near your cities (they ward the blight)
  • Build farmsteads for food so you can recruit
  • Research your Lord's first art at the Town Hall
  • Don't venture deep into the blight until you have a strong army

Stat Quick Reference ​

How Stats Grow ​

  • Leveling in battle: gain XP for damage dealt and kills (free, risky)
  • Paid training: spend gold at a barracks/hall; only charges for remaining XP

Combat Modifier Quick List ​

What adds damage:

  • Higher ground: +10% per height level (max +30%)
  • Flanking enemy: +25%
  • Behind enemy: +50%
  • Charging 2+ tiles: bonus damage
  • Rally active: +2 attack

What reduces damage:

  • Cover in forest/swamp: enemy harder to hit
  • Enemy at higher ground: −10% per height
  • Morale 0: −2 attack (after ally dies)
  • Shield Wall active: +3 defense
