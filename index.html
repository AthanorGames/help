How to Play
🗺️

A globe at war

Seven guilds ring Entropy's vast central blight and fight inward. The world wraps in every direction, so you can circle it. The deeper you press, the harder the fights and the finer the treasure.

⚔️

Battles the ground decides

Fight on an isometric grid where height, flanking and which way a soldier is facing all change the blow. Spring ambushes, storm a hold floor by floor, descend into the dark. Every fight is yours to win.

👑

Heroes worth raising

Level your Lord, promote champions, collect artifacts, learn skills, tame the alchemical bestiary. And keep everyone paid, or else.
